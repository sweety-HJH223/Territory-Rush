FIELD GUIDE

Claim territory. Outplay your rival.

Walk over a pickup to collect it.

How to play: Move with WASD. Leave your color to draw a trail, then return to your territory to close the loop and claim the enclosed tiles. Your trail is exposed until you return.

↯Speed BoostMove faster for 5 seconds
◇ShieldBlocks one trail-cut penalty
❄Trail-FreezeStops the rival from drawing a trail for 3 seconds
+Territory BonusClaims up to 9 nearby unclaimed tiles
!Trail danger: outside your color, your trail is exposed. If your rival touches it, you lose part of your territory (8% on Easy, 12% on Normal, 18% on Hard) and respawn at base. A Shield blocks one hit.
BREACH CAPTUREA loop can claim up to 9% of the arena at once. If it crosses rival land, it can steal outer cells (4 on Easy, 6 on Normal, 10 on Hard); bases stay protected.
THE CROWNActivates at 1:30 and moves at 0:45, always to a spot equally far from both bases. Stand on its tile for 5 seconds to score 1 Crown point. Each point adds 4 tiles to your final score.
