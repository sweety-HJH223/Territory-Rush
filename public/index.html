QUICK START

Move with WASD. Leave your color, draw a loop, then return to your color to claim the tiles inside. After 4 seconds your trail hardens into a wall your rival can’t cross or cut. From 0:30 your echo replays your moves from 30 seconds ago, claiming land and cutting rivals for you.

FIELD GUIDE

Claim territory. Outplay your rival.

Walk over a pickup to collect it.

How to play: Move with WASD. Leave your color to draw a trail, then return to your territory to close the loop and claim the enclosed tiles. Fresh trail can be cut; after 4 seconds it hardens into a wall for a while.

↯Speed BoostMove faster for 5 seconds
◇ShieldBlocks one trail-cut penalty
❄Trail-FreezeStops the rival from drawing a trail for 3 seconds
+Territory BonusClaims up to 9 nearby unclaimed tiles
↺RewindYour echo returns now, 10 seconds behind you
⊘Echo LockBreaks the rival's echo and blocks their next wave
!Trail danger: a fresh trail is exposed. If your rival touches it, you lose part of your territory (8% on Easy, 12% on Normal, 18% on Hard) and respawn at base. A Shield blocks one hit.
ECHO GHOSTSFrom 0:30, every 30 seconds each player gets an echo that replays exactly what they did 30 seconds earlier. Your echo draws trails, claims land for you, and cuts your rival’s trail if it runs into it. Touch your rival’s echo trail to break their echo until the next wave. Breaking your echo costs you nothing.
TRAILS HARDEN4 seconds after you draw a trail tile, it hardens into a solid block. For 6 seconds your rival can’t walk through it or cut it, so use it to block corridors. Then it cracks and becomes exposed again, and it flickers just before it cracks. Echo trails harden too.
BREACH CAPTUREA loop can claim up to 9% of the arena at once. If it crosses rival land, it can steal outer cells (4 on Easy, 6 on Normal, 10 on Hard); bases stay protected.
THE CROWNActivates at 1:30 and moves at 0:45, always to a spot equally far from both bases. Stand on its tile for 5 seconds to score 1 Crown point. Each point adds 4 tiles to your final score.
WASDMOVE
Close the loop to claim. Fresh trails can be cut; hardened trails block your rival.
